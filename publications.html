Peer-Reviewed Papers

Exploring Parallel Implementation of SPHINCS+ using Advanced Vector Extensions (AVX) Sets

Kavin Rajasekaran, Qian Wang

25th International Symposium on Quality Electronic Design (ISQED), 2025

Accepted

This paper presents a comprehensive study on parallelizing SPHINCS+ digital signature algorithms using AVX512 instruction sets. Our implementation achieved a 56% runtime reduction over the AVX2 baseline, demonstrating significant performance improvements for post-quantum cryptographic operations.
